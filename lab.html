(Lab) — small experiments

Things I try before they become websites.

Little pieces of motion, type and light. Each one is live, so play with it. The best ones end up in client sites as a hero, a menu or a booking page.

01 — Shader

Liquid light

A soft field of colour that leans towards your pointer or finger. It runs on the graphics card, so it stays smooth.

Could become: a hero for a cocktail bar, where the colours match the drinks.

Drag and throw

02 — Physics

A menu that falls

Menu items drop into the frame and pile up. Grab one and throw it. Tap the frame to drop them again.

Could become: a playful “today's specials” block for a bakery.

Fresh bread

03 — Type

Type with weight

Letters near the pointer grow heavier and taller, as if you were pressing dough.

Could become: the big name on a bakery or patisserie homepage.
