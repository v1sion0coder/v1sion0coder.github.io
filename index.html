(How it works)

Simple, step by step

You don't need to know anything about websites or computers. You tell me about your business in your own words, and I take care of everything else.

  1. 1

    You tell me about your business

    Fill in a short order form or chat with the Brief helper. It takes about five minutes. No payment.

  2. 2

    I send you a proposal

    Within two working days you get my ideas, the price and the timing. You decide in peace, with no obligation.

  3. 3

    I design and build your site

    You see previews along the way and can ask for changes. We talk in a private chat, by email or by phone.

  4. 4

    Your site goes live

    I put it online and show you how to use your own control panel: change prices, opening hours, photos and see orders.

(Questions)

Good to know

Do I need to understand computers or websites?

No. You tell me what your business does, in your own words. I handle the design, the technical work and putting it online. Afterwards I show you, step by step, how to change things yourself.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your site needs to do. Send an order (it's free and you are not committing to anything) and I reply with a clear price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Usually a few weeks, depending on the size of the site. You get the exact timing in my proposal.

Can I change texts, prices or photos later myself?

Yes. Every site comes with a simple control panel where you can update your information, opening hours and prices, and see orders or bookings.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Most people visit websites on their phone, so every site is designed for phones first, and works on tablets and computers too.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's completely fine. Just tell me about your business and what you'd like to achieve, and I'll suggest what makes sense for you.

How do I contact you?

Use the order page: you can chat with the Brief helper or fill in the form yourself. After sending, you get a private page where you can write to me directly.
