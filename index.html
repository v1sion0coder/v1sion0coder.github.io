(02) Process

How we work together

You don't need to know anything about websites. You tell me about your business in your own words; I take care of the rest and keep you in the loop at every step.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    You send a short brief (about five minutes, free, no obligation). I read it and ask what I need to know.

  2. 02

    Plan & price

    Within about two working days: the package that fits, any add-ons, the fixed price and the timing. You decide in peace.

  3. 03

    Direction

    I shape the look: typography, colours, layout. You see it early, before anything is built.

  4. 04

    Build & refine

    I build the site and share previews in your private chat. Rounds of changes are agreed in your package.

  5. 05

    Launch

    I put it online on the hosting and domain you choose, test it on real phones and show you how everything works.

(03) Work

Selected
work

Three working concept sites, each designed for a real café as an unsolicited proposal. They are shown under project names until the owners agree to be named. Photos are licensed stock placeholders; everything else is real, running work.

01 — Riga · 2026

Concept · self-initiated · fully built

Layers

Honey-cake & pastry house

A pastry house with two sides: French viennoiserie and Latvian layer cakes. The whole site is built around one object, the cake slice.

The idea
Scroll and the 3D slice opens into its layers; each layer is a part of the house: cakes, viennoiserie, ordering, visiting.
What works
Cake pre-orders with pickup times, a staff admin with orders, products and stats, two-step sign-in, Latvian and English.
  • 3D hero
  • Pre-orders
  • Admin
  • LV / EN

02 — Tallinn · 2026

Concept · self-initiated · fully built

Grid

Gluten-free waffle café

A small café where every waffle is gluten-free. The goal: make booking a table take three taps and make the menu feel warm.

The idea
A 3D waffle you can spin and dress with your own toppings, so the first screen already feels like ordering.
What works
Table booking with live free times, guests manage their own booking by private link, a waitlist, staff roles with two-step sign-in, menu editing and stats.
  • 3D hero
  • Table booking
  • Waitlist
  • Staff roles

03 — Helsinki · 2026

Concept · self-initiated · fully built

Rye

Karelian bakery

A traditional Karelian bakery. No 3D here on purpose: the character comes from the craft itself.

The idea
Rypytys, the pinched edge of a Karelian pie, drawn in code and used for frames, dividers and buttons across the site.
What works
Orders for parties by the dozen, opening hours that know if the shop is open right now, an admin for orders, Finnish and English.
  • Hand-drawn system
  • Party orders

  • FI / EN

What clients say

(Questions)

Good to know

Do I need to understand computers or websites?

No. You tell me what your business does, in your own words. I handle the design, the technical work and putting it online. Afterwards I show you, step by step, how to change things yourself.

How much does a website cost?

Starter is €150, Business €250 and Signature €350. One fixed price for the website, paid once. Hosting is not included; I help you choose a simple option. Sending an order is free and you are not committing to anything: nothing is paid until you say yes.

How long does it take?

It depends on the package and on when your texts and photos are ready. You get the timing in my plan, before anything starts.

Can I change texts, prices or photos later myself?

If you change things often, add the admin panel: then you edit menu, prices and hours yourself. Otherwise just send me the changes; I always tell you the price before doing a paid update.

Can I get online ordering or table booking?

Yes, as an add-on on top of a package. I have built pre-orders, table booking with a waitlist and staff admin panels (see the work above). You get a fixed quote for exactly what you need.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Most people visit websites on their phone, so every site is designed for phones first, and works on tablets and computers too.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's completely fine. Just tell me about your business and what you'd like to achieve, and I'll suggest what makes sense for you.

How do I contact you?

Use the project page: you can chat with the Brief helper or fill in the form yourself. You can also message me on Telegram (@v1sion_TT). After sending an order, you get a private page where you can write to me directly.
