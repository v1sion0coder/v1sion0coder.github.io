No templates. Every place has its own smell, its own sound, its own regulars. Its website should feel like walking through its door.

(01)

What I do

  1. 01

    Art direction & design

    A visual world made for one place only: type, colour, motion and 3D that tell its story. Never a template.

  2. 02

    Online orders & pre‑orders

    Cakes, pastries and boxes ordered ahead, with pickup times and daily limits the kitchen can actually keep.

  3. 03

    Table booking

    Guests book in a few taps and manage their own booking. No phone tag, fewer no‑shows.

  4. 04

    Owner admin panel

    Orders, bookings, menu and opening hours in one place. Staff roles and two-factor login built in.

  5. 05

    Phone-first, fast & secure

    Most guests arrive on a phone, so that is where it is designed first. Quick to load, careful with data.

  6. 06

    Bilingual from day one

    Local language and English side by side, for regulars and visitors alike.
