Layers

Honey-cake & pastry house

A pastry house with two sides: French viennoiserie and Latvian layer cakes. The whole site is built around one object, the slice of honey cake.

Status
Concept, self-initiated, fully built
Role
Design and development, by v1sion
Languages
Latvian / English
Year
2026

01The concept

“Layers”. A honey cake is made of thin layers; so is the house: a French counter and a Latvian counter, cakes and pastries, ordering and visiting. The site turns that into structure. Scroll on the homepage and the 3D slice opens, and each layer becomes a door to one part of the house.

02Who it's for

Neighbours and office workers who drop in, and people ordering a whole cake for a birthday or a family event. Many read in Latvian, visitors in English, and almost everyone on a phone.

03UX decisions

  • One clear action everywhere: “Order a cake”, with the pickup date and a two-hour window chosen in a few taps.
  • Minimum notice and a daily kitchen limit are checked while ordering, so nobody orders a cake that can't be made.
  • A private link lets the guest change or cancel their own order, without phone calls.
  • The menu is split into the two counters, so the house's identity is clear in seconds.

04What works

  • Cake pre-orders: cake, size, quantity, pickup day and window, inscription, notes, allergies (stored only with consent)
  • Private manage / cancel link and a calendar file for the guest
  • Back office: orders by day and week, printable bake list, phone and counter orders, stop online orders for a day
  • Products, limits, opening hours and menu editor; stats from one day to one year
  • Owner and staff roles, two-step sign-in, security log

05How it's built

  • Three.js for the 3D cake slice, GSAP for scroll motion
  • Node.js + Express server with a SQLite database
  • Strict content security policy, rate limits, hashed passwords and tokens
  • Bilingual content (LV / EN) with the visitor's choice remembered

06On the phone

About this project. Layers is a concept: designed and built by V1SION as a proposal for an existing honey-cake & pastry house in Riga, not a commissioned job.
