Rye

Karelian bakery

A traditional Karelian bakery. No 3D here, on purpose: the character comes from the craft itself.

Status
Concept, self-initiated, fully built
Role
Design and development, by v1sion
Languages
Finnish / English
Year
2026

01The concept

“Rypytys”, the pinched edge of a Karelian pie. The crimped line is drawn in code and becomes the visual system: frames around photos, dividers, buttons and the loading shape. Warm paper colours and a soft serif keep it homely, not trendy.

02Who it's for

Regulars who want to know what is on the counter today, and families ordering pies by the dozen for a party, people of all ages, mostly on a phone and in Finnish first.

03UX decisions

  • The homepage says right away whether the shop is open now and when it opens next.
  • “In the counter today” shows the usual range with prices, so people know before they come.
  • Ordering by the dozen for a party: product, amount, a pickup day and a two-hour window.
  • Finnish first, English one tap away; big, calm type for easy reading.

04What works

  • Pickup pre-orders for pies, buns and cakes, with minimum notice and a daily kitchen limit
  • Private manage / cancel link and a calendar file
  • Back office: orders by day and week, bake list, phone and counter orders
  • Products, limits, opening hours and menu editor; stats; owner and staff roles with two-step sign-in
  • Bilingual emails, Finnish first

05How it's built

  • Hand-drawn SVG crimp system, no images needed for the decoration
  • Node.js + Express server with a SQLite database
  • All times in Helsinki time, whatever the server's timezone
  • Strict content security policy, rate limits, hashed passwords and tokens

06On the phone

About this project. Rye is a concept: designed and built by V1SION as a proposal for an existing karelian bakery in Helsinki, not a commissioned job. It is shown under a project name until the owner agrees to be named. Photos are licensed stock placeholders. A live demo can be shown on a call.
